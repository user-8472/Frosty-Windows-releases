# Frosty Windows

Frosty Windows tells you the night before if you'll need to scrape frost off your car in the
morning. On frosty mornings, it can ring an extra alarm a set time before you need to start
scraping. Then you have time to scrape before you leave. It's free, with no ads and no tracking.
No account needed.

> **Android phones only.** There's no iPhone version.

## Get it from Google Play (testing now)

Frosty Windows is being tested on Google Play before it opens to everyone. Joining takes three
steps. Do them all on your Android phone, signed in with **the Google account you use on that
phone**.

1. **Join the testers group.** Open
   **[the testers group](https://groups.google.com/g/frosty-windows-testers)** and tap
   **Join group**. You won't get emails from it. Does it say **"You don't have permission"**? Then
   you're not signed in. Tap **Sign in** at the top right, with the account your Play Store uses.
   Or open the link in Chrome.
2. **Become a tester.** Open **[Google's test page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.frostywindows.app)**
   and tap **Become a tester**.
3. **Install it** from the Play Store:
   **[Frosty Windows on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.frostywindows.app)**.
   Google Play keeps it up to date after that. You can't find it by searching the Play Store
   until the test is over.

Does step 3 say **"Item not found"**? Wait a few minutes, then try again. After step 2, it can take a
few minutes, sometimes longer, before a new tester can see the app. Still not there after about half
an hour? The Play Store may be using a different Google account from the one you used in steps 1
and 2. Tap your profile picture in the Play Store and switch accounts. More help:
[Trouble getting set up?](https://user-8472.github.io/Frosty-Windows-releases/#join-trouble).
Still stuck? Email **frostywindowsapp@gmail.com** with the Google account on your phone, and you'll
be added to the test.

Already using the copy from this page? Join the test the same way. Installing from Google Play keeps
your alerts and settings.

## Or install it directly

Don't want to wait? Install it from this page instead. It takes a few more taps than a Play Store
app, about two minutes. The steps are below.

### Install steps

Do these steps **on your Android phone** (not on a computer).

1. Tap **[Download Frosty Windows](https://github.com/user-8472/Frosty-Windows-releases/releases/latest/download/frosty-windows.apk)**.
2. Your browser may warn that the file **might be harmful**. It shows this for any app that doesn't come
   from the Play Store. Tap **Download anyway**.
3. When the download finishes, tap **Open**. (Missed it? Open the **Files** or **My Files** app, go to
   **Downloads**, and tap **frosty-windows.apk**.)
4. If you see **"For your security, your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this
   source"**: tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, then tap the back arrow.
5. Tap **Install**.
6. **Google Play Protect** may stop you with a message such as "Unsafe app blocked" or "Send app for
   scanning?". It says this because it hasn't seen Frosty Windows in the Play Store. Tap
   **More details**, then **Install anyway**. Or tap **Scan app**, then **Install**.
7. Tap **Open**.

#### Samsung phones: Auto Blocker

Newer Samsung phones have **Auto Blocker**, which blocks every app that isn't from the Play Store or
the Galaxy Store. If the install is blocked and the message mentions Auto Blocker:

1. Open **Settings** → **Security and privacy** → **Auto Blocker**, and switch it **off**.
2. Install Frosty Windows (the steps above).
3. You can switch Auto Blocker back on afterward. If a later update is blocked, switch it off again
   while the update installs.

## Set it up

The first time you open Frosty Windows, it walks you through setting up an alert. You choose where
your car is parked overnight, when you leave and on which days, and how you want to hear about frost.
Then the app asks for a few permissions, so it can warn you on time:

- **Notifications**: for frost notifications. Alarms need it too, because they ring through a
  notification.
- **“Alarms & reminders”**: so alarms and notifications come on time. Without it, your phone may
  delay them for hours while it sleeps.
- **Full-screen alarms** (only if you choose an alarm): so a frost alarm can wake your lock screen
  with Snooze and Dismiss, like a clock alarm.
- **Unrestricted battery use** (only on some phones): if your phone skips the app's overnight
  forecast checks, the home screen asks for this later. It stops the phone from putting Frosty
  Windows to sleep at night.

If any of these are still off, the home screen shows a **Finish setting up** card. It lists the steps
left, with a button for the next one.

## Updates

The copy from this page checks for new versions by itself (**⋮ menu → Settings → Updates**).
Updates install over the old version and keep your alerts and settings.

- The first time it updates, Android may ask if Frosty Windows can **install apps**. Switch on
  **Allow from this source**, then go back.
- Play Protect may ask to scan each new version. Tap **Scan app** or **Install anyway**, as above.

## If something goes wrong

- **"App not installed"**: an older copy, installed a different way, is in the way. Uninstall
  Frosty Windows (press and hold its icon → **Uninstall**). Then install it again. You'll need to set
  up your alerts again.
- **Can't find the downloaded file**: open the **Files** (or **My Files**) app → **Downloads**, or in
  Chrome tap **⋮** → **Downloads**.
- **Opened this page on a computer**: send yourself the link, and open it on your phone instead.
- **The alarm didn't ring**: open the app and look for a card on the home screen about permissions
  or battery. Then tap the alert, tap **Edit**, and tap **Test the alarm** to hear it.

## Privacy

No ads, no tracking and no account. Your alerts and settings stay on your phone. The app only goes
online to:

- get forecasts for your alerts' places from Open-Meteo (and, as a backup, from the US National
  Weather Service);
- look up a place name you search for (Open-Meteo), or, if you tap **Use my current location**, ask your
  phone's built-in address lookup (usually Google's) for the name of that spot;
- check GitHub for new versions of the app, and download them (the copy installed from this page
  only).

The full details: [Privacy policy](https://user-8472.github.io/Frosty-Windows-releases/privacy).
