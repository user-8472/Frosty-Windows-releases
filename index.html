How it works

Set it up once. The app does the rest.

On a clear night, four things decide if glass frosts over: temperature, dew point, cloud cover and wind. Frosty Windows watches them for you.

  1. Set up an alert

    Tell the app where your car is parked overnight, when you leave and on which days. Then choose how you want to hear about frost.

  2. The app checks the forecast

    It reads the hour-by-hour forecast for your place. It checks the forecast again about every two hours, overnight too.

  3. You hear about it in time

    The night before, you get a notification. On frosty mornings, you get a notification before you leave, an extra alarm before you need to start scraping, or both. You choose.

📲 Install it directly

Don't want to wait? Install it from this page instead. It takes a few more taps than a Play Store app, about two minutes.

📱Do these steps on your Android phone, not on a computer.
  1. Tap Download Frosty Windows.
  2. Your browser may warn that the file might be harmful. It shows this for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.
  3. When the download finishes, tap Open. (Missed it? Open the Files or My Files app, go to Downloads, and tap frosty-windows.apk.)

Set it up

The first time you open Frosty Windows, it walks you through setting up an alert. You choose where your car is parked overnight, when you leave and on which days, and how you want to hear about frost. Then the app asks for a few permissions, so it can warn you on time:

  • Notifications: for frost notifications. Alarms need it too, because they ring through a notification.
  • “Alarms & reminders”: so alarms and notifications come on time. Without it, your phone may delay them for hours while it sleeps.
  • Full-screen alarms (only if you choose an alarm): so a frost alarm can wake your lock screen with Snooze and Dismiss, like a clock alarm.
  • Unrestricted battery use (only on some phones): if your phone skips the app's overnight forecast checks, the home screen asks for this later. It stops the phone from putting Frosty Windows to sleep at night.

If any of these are still off, the home screen shows a Finish setting up card. It lists the steps left, with a button for the next one.

Updates

The copy from this page checks for new versions by itself (⋮ menu → Settings → Updates). Updates install over the old version and keep your alerts and settings.

  • The first time it updates, Android may ask if Frosty Windows can install apps. Switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  • Play Protect may ask to scan each new version. Tap Scan app or Install anyway, as above.
If something goes wrong
  • "App not installed": an older copy, installed a different way, is in the way. Uninstall Frosty Windows (press and hold its icon → Uninstall). Then install it again. You'll need to set up your alerts again.
  • Can't find the downloaded file: open the Files (or My Files) app → Downloads, or in Chrome tap ⋮ → Downloads.
  • Opened this page on a computer: send yourself the link, and open it on your phone instead.
  • The alarm didn't ring: open the app and look for a card on the home screen about permissions or battery. Then tap the alert, tap Edit, and tap Test the alarm to hear it.

Privacy

Your alerts stay on your phone

🔒

No ads, no tracking and no account

Frosty Windows has no server and no account. Your alerts, settings and the forecasts the app downloads are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

The app only goes online to

  • get forecasts for your alerts' places from Open-Meteo (and, as a backup, from the US National Weather Service);
  • look up a place name you search for (Open-Meteo), or, if you tap Use my current location, ask your phone's built-in address lookup (usually Google's) for the name of that spot;
  • check GitHub for new versions of the app, and download them (the copy installed from this page only).
